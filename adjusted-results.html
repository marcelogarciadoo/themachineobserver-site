A separate research view of demographic treatment and residual standardization before the election-day projection.

Experimental research output

10 Datafolha waves receive the evidence-supported five-variable reconstruction; the other 85 retain their published demographic composition. Residual institute deviations are standardized across all waves. Predictive intervals were recalibrated with leave-one-election-round-out replays, but only three independent historical contests are available and the PNAD treatment itself could not be replayed. These figures are visible for research review, not approved for production publication. Read the full methodology.

Inputs and audit trail

  • 95 runoff waves retained: 10 supported Datafolha demographic reconstructions and 85 explicit no-adjustment statuses.
  • Residual standardization is stored separately from PNAD treatment for every wave.
  • 12 historical cutoffs: recalibrated predictive coverage 83%, versus 33% for the original narrow intervals.
  • Turnout, unresolved-voter and correlation assumptions remain documented in the methodology and versioned research artifact; they are not presented as a second headline chart.

The election-day Monte Carlo distribution is shown on Predict victory, while prediction markets remain entirely outside this pipeline. Production approval remains blocked by the small number of independent elections and the absence of historical demographic replay.
