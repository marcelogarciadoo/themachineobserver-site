Why correct the polls?

Historical misses can be materially larger than published sampling margins. A poll can differ from the final result because of institute design, coverage, nonresponse, question effects, turnout and genuine campaign movement. Public data do not reliably separate those causes. The operating method therefore measures one comparable, institute-specific historical miss at the same point in the cycle and applies it transparently—without claiming it is a permanent causal bias.

Current status: matched-horizon adjustments and paired raw/corrected Monte Carlo are active as research outputs. Only 2022 supplies a matched historical election, so the simulation is not described as independently validated. PNAD, residual institute effects and prediction markets are not inputs.

01 · Define the target

National election, round, candidate scenario, denominator and cutoff. The runoff target is Lula versus Flávio; polls, current opinion and election-day outcomes are distinct quantities.

02 · Preserve source evidence

Archive publication and fieldwork dates, sample, mode, question, candidate list, margin, confidence, registration, URL and hash. Keep institute separate from sponsor.

03 · Normalize without invention

Deduplicate waves, preserve every denominator and missing category, and never turn missing values into zero. First- and second-round scenarios remain linked by wave and registration.

04 · Show the raw baseline

Display published points and a descriptive trailing seven-day average. Dotted continuity holds the last displayed average through empty windows; it is not imputed data or a forecast.

05 · Build eligible historical pairs

For each 2026 wave, find a 2022 wave from the same institute containing both a first-round scenario and the Lula–right-candidate runoff. Both rounds must come from that one historical wave.

06 · Match election horizons

Compare days remaining to the first and second rounds in 2026 with days remaining in 2022. Both differences must be at most 14 days; first-round proximity breaks ties because today precedes that vote.

07 · Convert to valid votes

Within each runoff wave, divide Lula and the right candidate by their two-candidate total. Apply the same valid-vote denominator to the current poll, historical poll and official result.

08 · Calculate candidate errors

For each candidate, error = historical poll valid share − official 2022 valid share. The correction is the signed opposite: official result − historical poll. Direction is retained; “absolute” means candidate-specific rather than margin-only.

09 · Apply the correction

Adjusted 2026 share = current valid-vote share + candidate-specific historical correction. Lula and Flávio remain complementary. No PNAD, demographic reconstruction or residual institute layer is added.

10 · Validate and freeze

Reject mismatched registrations, out-of-tolerance horizons, denominator failures or shares outside 0–100. Store raw, matched wave, formula, output, cutoff, version and hashes.

11 · Define paired simulation inputs

Select one latest eligible wave per canonical institute and give institutes equal weight. The raw center averages its published two-candidate valid-vote shares; the corrected center averages those same waves after the signed matched-horizon correction. AtlasIntel labels are consolidated before selection.

12 · Calibrate and run Monte Carlo

Estimate current-center uncertainty as the larger of the cross-institute standard error and the published-margin sampling floor. Predictive error is the RMS of the selected 2022 matched errors around zero. Apply that same full scale to both models: with only one historical election, shifting the corrected center does not justify shrinking its uncertainty. Convert to log-odds, set unmeasured current/future correlation to zero, then generate 50,000 deterministic paired draws with one fixed seed. Every draw produces complementary Lula–Flávio valid-vote shares.

13 · Check, label and compare

Verify identities, bounds, deterministic replay, paired inputs, intervals and histogram totals. The two histograms and fitted Gaussian curves share one 30–70% axis. Because only 2022 supplies an independent matched election, the result is labelled research-only with limited historical calibration; prediction markets remain outside the model.

14 · Publish an auditable release

Publish inputs, exclusions, code/configuration versions, matched pairs, parameters, hashes and change log. Prediction markets retain separate timestamps, contracts and settlement rules and never enter the poll correction.

How the offenders ranking is calculated

Canonicalize institute aliases, take the latest eligible 2026 wave for each institute and display the signed 2022 poll-minus-result error selected by that match. Rank its absolute runoff magnitude. In a two-candidate valid-vote comparison, both candidates have the same absolute error, so summing them would double-count one miss. The table retains both signs, dates, registrations and horizon gaps. No PNAD, residual or margin-of-error subtraction is used.
