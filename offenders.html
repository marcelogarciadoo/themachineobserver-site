Historical polling error by institute

For each certified election round, the calculation selects the latest eligible total-respondent poll published within 14 days of the vote, measures the absolute difference between its Lula–opponent margin and the official result, then averages those absolute errors by institute. The chart shows only that total historical error—no PNAD, residual or margin-of-error breakdown.

How to read the residual

The residual model estimates systematic margin error not explained by the available demographic correction. It is stored separately from PNAD and used only as a research standardization. Historical demographic inputs could not be replayed consistently, so the present residual may still contain demographic effects.
