Matched 2022 runoff error by institute

For each canonical institute, its latest eligible 2026 wave selects one 2022 wave from that institute containing both first-round and runoff scenarios. Both election horizons must be within ±14 days. The ranking is the absolute signed miss for either runoff candidate; complementary valid-vote shares make the two magnitudes identical.

What the correction contains

It deliberately bundles the historical institute miss and genuine campaign movement between the matched poll and election day. It is therefore a matched-horizon historical correction—not a causal house-bias estimate. The same latest eligible institute waves also feed the raw and corrected Monte Carlo comparison.
