Brazil presidential polling database: first and second rounds

Browse individual Brazil presidential polling records by round, institute and fieldwork date. Search, inspect and export illustrative records.

The database separates first-round and second-round scenarios and preserves each poll’s original percentages, sample size, interview mode and fieldwork dates.

Search and institute filters apply to the records and CSV export. Production records will link the original report, TSE registration and correction history. All polling records in this prototype are synthetic.
