Brazil polling accuracy: historical errors and institute comparisons

Understand polling-institute errors and compare latest raw polls with same-date corrected Observer estimates. Fictional scorecards in this prototype.

Historical accuracy needs matched election horizons, denominators and certified results. Scorecards separate absolute error, signed bias, interval coverage and sample coverage.
