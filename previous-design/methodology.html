Election forecast methodology: polls, PNAD, bias and uncertainty

Read the proposed Brazil election forecasting methodology: poll normalization, PNAD and TSE benchmarks, house effects, backtesting and uncertainty.

The proposed framework preserves raw polls, normalizes comparable scenarios and estimates pollster effects. PNAD/IBGE and TSE targets must match the relevant survey population; marginal tables alone do not support arbitrary multivariate reweighting.

Historical backtests use only evidence available at each cutoff. Forecast simulations must include correlated polling errors, turnout, undecided voters and future movement. Versioned inputs and assumptions support an auditable record; markets remain separate.
